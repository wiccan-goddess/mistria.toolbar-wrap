Reason:

No discredit to the mod author and I'm not trying to steal their credit or anything like that. I just simply don't like the fact that AI made it. Yes, figuring out how to mod gml files is probably going to be hard but I'm down to struggle if it helps encourage others to make mods without relying on generative AI. Now that's not to say AI doesn't have a place in the modding community because it might help some people understand how a mod was/is made. That still doesn't dismiss the fact that generative AI is BAD for EVERYONE not just artists or content creators.
